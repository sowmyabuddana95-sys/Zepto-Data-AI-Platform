Returns & Refunds

Groceries and perishables can be returned within 24 hours if damaged, spoiled, or incorrect. Non-perishables can be returned within 7 days if unopened and resalable. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days or instantly to the Zepto wallet. Opened personal care products are non-returnable except for manufacturing defects. Return pickup is free.
